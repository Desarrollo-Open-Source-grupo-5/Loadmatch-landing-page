Frequently asked questions

Publishing requests is free for companies. We charge a commission on the confirmed service price, shown transparently before you accept.

We currently operate across Metropolitan Lima and Callao. We are gradually expanding to other regions of Peru.

We verify MTC authorisation, current SOAT insurance, technical inspection and driving licence against official records before a carrier can quote.

You can report the incident from the application. Our support team follows up on the case and records it in the carrier's history.

General packaged goods, palletised loads and dry merchandise. Hazardous, perishable and oversized cargo are not supported yet.
