1. Terms of service

These terms govern access to and use of the LoadMatch platform, which connects companies that need to move goods with carriers that have available capacity. By creating an account you accept these terms in full.

1.1 Role of the platform

LoadMatch acts as an intermediary that facilitates contact, quoting and tracking between companies and carriers. LoadMatch does not own vehicles and does not carry out transport services directly. The transport contract is entered into between the company and the carrier.

1.2 Carrier verification

Before a carrier can submit quotes, The proposed workflow includes review of carrier documentation; automatic checks against official records depend on available integrations and are not demonstrated by this prototype. Verification does not constitute a guarantee of performance; responsibility for the service lies with the contracted carrier.

1.3 Fees

Publishing a cargo request is free of charge. LoadMatch charges a commission on the price of a confirmed service. The applicable rate is displayed before the company accepts a quote. There are no membership fees or account maintenance charges.

1.4 Acceptable use

  • Provide truthful and up-to-date information about your company, vehicle and documentation.
  • Do not use the platform to move hazardous, illegal or undeclared goods.
  • Do not attempt to circumvent the platform to avoid the applicable commission.

1.5 Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the Republic of Peru. Any dispute shall be submitted to the courts of Lima.

2. Privacy policy

We collect only the data required to operate the service: company identification (RUC and registered name), contact details, vehicle and documentation data for carriers, and the operational records of each shipment.

2.1 Use of data

  • To match cargo requests with compatible carriers.
  • To verify documentation against official MTC and SUNAT records.
  • To issue receipts and settlement documents.
  • To send notifications about quotes and shipment status.

2.2 Your rights

Under Peruvian Personal Data Protection Law No. 29733 you may access, rectify, cancel or object to the processing of your personal data by writing to hola@loadmatch.pe.

2.3 Data sharing

We share the minimum data necessary between the parties to a shipment. We do not sell personal data to third parties.

3. Cookie policy

This landing page uses only strictly necessary local storage to remember your language preference. It does not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

The video placeholders reference youtube-nocookie.com. External fonts and video embeds may contact their providers. Their behaviour must be reviewed in the published release before claiming that no third-party tracking occurs.
